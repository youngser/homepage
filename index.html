Research Interests

My research focuses on statistical machine learning and inference for graphs and networks, with applications in neuroscience (connectomics), language processing, and other domains. Topics include:

  • Spectral methods for graph inference and clustering
  • Nonparametric and semiparametric statistical models for networks
  • Random dot product graphs (RDPG) and stochastic block models (SBM)
  • Vertex nomination and graph matching
  • Dimensionality estimation and model selection for graph data
